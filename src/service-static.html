What to expect

Our journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we discuss your aesthetic goals and assess your facial structure. Dr. Qneibi will explain the treatment process, expected results, and answer all your questions to ensure you feel confident and informed.

During your appointment, we'll carefully mark the treatment areas and may apply a topical numbing cream for your comfort. Using ultra-fine needles, your provider will precisely inject the Botox or dermal filler into targeted locations. The entire procedure typically takes 15-30 minutes, with minimal discomfort.

Who it's for

Botox and dermal fillers are ideal for adults looking to address signs of aging without surgery. Whether you're noticing your first fine lines or seeking to restore lost volume, these treatments can help you achieve a refreshed, more youthful appearance.

Who can benefit

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Loss of facial volume
  • Crow's feet around eyes
  • Forehead lines and frown lines
  • Thin or aging lips
  • Nasolabial folds (smile lines)
