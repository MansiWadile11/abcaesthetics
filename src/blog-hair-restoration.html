Assessment first: how a hair restoration plan is decided

Hair loss has more than one cause, and the pattern matters. That is why a plan follows an assessment rather than the other way round.

1. The pattern shapes the plan

Your hair-loss pattern and overall clinical history are assessed first, and the plan is built around what that assessment finds rather than applied as a standard protocol.

2. PRP hair restoration

An individualized approach using platelet-rich plasma derived from your own blood, offered for selected hair-thinning concerns following assessment.

3. Regenerative and peptide-based approaches

Where clinically appropriate, selected regenerative or peptide-based approaches may be incorporated into an individualized hair-restoration plan.

4. What can honestly be said about results

No specific outcome is guaranteed. Results vary by individual, and what is realistic for your particular pattern of hair loss is discussed openly at consultation.

We do not make claims about guaranteed hair growth or reversal of hair loss, and you should be cautious of anywhere that does.

“An individualized PRP-based approach for selected hair-thinning concerns following assessment of the patient’s hair-loss pattern and overall clinical history.”

Conclusion

The most useful thing a consultation gives you here is a realistic picture — of what is causing the thinning, and of what treatment can and cannot change.
