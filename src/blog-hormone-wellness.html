How a hormone and metabolic wellness plan is built

Hormone therapy and medical weight management are medical programmes, not treatments you book off a list. This is the order things happen in.

1. Evaluation comes first

Hormone therapy is based on appropriate medical evaluation, your symptoms, medical history, treatment goals, and an assessment of the risks and benefits for you specifically.

No programme is started without that evaluation.

2. Medical weight management

An individualized programme incorporating clinical evaluation, lifestyle and nutrition considerations, and medically appropriate therapies when indicated.

3. Where GLP-1 medications fit

Semaglutide and tirzepatide may be prescribed as part of an appropriate, medically supervised weight-management programme for suitable patients. Whether they are appropriate for you is a clinical decision made at consultation.

4. Peptide therapy

Individualized peptide-based services are selected according to the specific peptide, intended use, your goals, clinical considerations, the available evidence, and applicable regulatory requirements.

“Individualized hormone therapy services based on appropriate medical evaluation, symptoms, medical history, treatment goals, and assessment of risks and benefits.”

Conclusion

The consultation is the programme’s first step rather than a formality before it. What follows is built from what that assessment finds.
