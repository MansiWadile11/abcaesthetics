Overview

What is Regenerative Aesthetics?

Regenerative aesthetic treatments use components obtained from your own blood, prepared in the clinic and incorporated into selected individualized treatment plans.

PRP and PRF may be used within aesthetic, skin-rejuvenation, and hair-restoration plans where clinically appropriate.

Treatments

Regenerative treatments we offer

Product and treatment selection is individualized following consultation and clinical assessment.

A blood-derived treatment using components obtained from your own blood. PRP may be incorporated into selected aesthetic and hair-restoration treatment plans.

A blood-derived fibrin matrix containing platelets and other naturally occurring blood components. Unlike PRP, PRF uses no anticoagulants — the fibrin matrix releases growth factors gradually over 7–10 days. Used for under-eye hollows, fine lines and skin texture, alongside microneedling, or as an add-on to filler.

Potential regenerative and emerging aesthetic treatments involving applicable Kimera® or exosome-based products. This service is listed for completeness and is not currently offered for booking: the specific product, intended use, regulatory status and sourcing are being verified before it is made available.
