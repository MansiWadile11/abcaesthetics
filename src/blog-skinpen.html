What SkinPen® microneedling is cleared to treat

Microneedling is offered widely and described loosely. SkinPen® has specific FDA-cleared indications, and it is worth knowing what they are.

1. The cleared indications

SkinPen® is a microneedling device used for FDA-cleared indications, including improving the appearance of facial acne scars in adults and, for appropriate Fitzpatrick skin types, improving the appearance of neck wrinkles.

Treatment suitability is determined through individualized assessment.

2. How microneedling works

It is a minimally invasive skin-rejuvenation treatment that creates controlled micro-injuries to support the skin’s natural healing and collagen-remodeling response.

3. Adding PRP

PRP microneedling combines the treatment with platelet-rich plasma derived from your own blood, incorporated into selected individualized skin-rejuvenation treatment plans.

4. Deciding whether it suits you

Your skin type, the concern you want addressed and your relevant history all matter. Suitability is determined following individualized evaluation, and results vary by individual.

“SkinPen® is a microneedling device used for FDA-cleared indications. Treatment suitability is determined through individualized assessment.”

Conclusion

A cleared indication is a useful thing to ask about. It tells you what a device has been assessed for, rather than what it is marketed for.
