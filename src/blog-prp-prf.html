PRP and PRF: regenerative options for skin and hair

Both are prepared from a sample of your own blood, drawn and processed at your appointment. Here is the difference, and where each is used.

1. What they are

  • PRP — platelet-rich plasma, a blood-derived treatment using components obtained from your own blood.
  • PRF — platelet-rich fibrin, a fibrin matrix containing platelets and other naturally occurring blood components.

Which is used is decided as part of your individualized plan.

2. Where they are used

PRP may be incorporated into selected aesthetic and hair-restoration treatment plans. PRF is used in selected individualized aesthetic and regenerative plans. PRP is also combined with microneedling in some skin-rejuvenation plans.

3. The material comes from you

A small sample of your blood is drawn at the appointment and processed on site. Nothing is added from another source.

4. What about exosomes?

Kimera® and exosome-based products are an emerging area. Availability and suitability are confirmed at consultation, and we do not make claims about outcomes for these treatments.

“A blood-derived treatment using components obtained from the patient’s own blood. PRP may be incorporated into selected aesthetic and hair-restoration treatment plans.”

Conclusion

Regenerative treatments are part of a plan rather than a plan in themselves. Whether they are appropriate for your goals is determined following individualized evaluation.
