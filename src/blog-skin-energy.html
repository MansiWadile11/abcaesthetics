How SkinTyte®, SkinSmooth® and Sofwave™ differ

Three non-invasive treatments for skin quality and laxity, each addressing a different concern. Two share a platform and can be combined — here is what each one is for.

1. They target different things

  • SkinTyte® — a non-invasive light-based treatment designed to support the appearance of skin firmness and address selected concerns related to skin laxity.
  • SkinSmooth® — designed to support improvement in skin tone, texture and overall appearance.
  • Sofwave™ — an FDA-cleared ultrasound technology used for fine lines, wrinkles, and mild to moderate laxity of the face and neck. It works at a different depth from the two light-based treatments above, which is why it addresses a different concern rather than a stronger version of the same one.

2. They can be combined

SkinTyte® and SkinSmooth® may be used individually or strategically combined when clinically appropriate. Treatment selection and sequencing are individualized according to your skin concerns, skin type, treatment goals and clinical assessment.

Whether combining is right for you is a decision made at consultation, not a package chosen in advance.

3. Where microneedling fits

Microneedling is a separate, minimally invasive treatment that creates controlled micro-injuries to support the skin’s natural healing and collagen-remodeling response. It can be combined with platelet-rich plasma derived from your own blood.

SkinPen® is a microneedling device used for FDA-cleared indications, including improving the appearance of facial acne scars in adults and, for appropriate Fitzpatrick skin types, improving the appearance of neck wrinkles.

4. Suitability is assessed first

Skin type, skin concerns and relevant history all shape what is appropriate. Suitability is determined following individualized evaluation, and results vary by individual.

“Treatment selection and sequencing are individualized according to the patient’s skin concerns, skin type, treatment goals, and clinical assessment.”

Conclusion

These are tools, not products off a menu. The useful question at consultation is what you want your skin to do differently — the device follows from the answer.
