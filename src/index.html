During your consultation, Dr. Qneibi evaluates your goals, concerns, and medical history to recommend an individualized treatment plan.

The number of sessions depends on your goals and the treatment chosen. Most plans are built as a short series, and Dr. Qneibi will outline the schedule up front.

Most treatments involve minimal discomfort. Numbing options are available and we check in with you throughout the procedure.

Botox® relaxes the muscles that create expression lines, while dermal fillers restore lost volume and contour. Many plans use both, and Dr. Qneibi will explain which suits your goals.

Your first visit is an unhurried consultation — a review of your goals and history, an evaluation, and a treatment plan built around you.
